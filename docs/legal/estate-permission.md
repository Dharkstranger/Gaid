## 3. What the copy will say, and how

The signer agrees, on behalf of the estate, that:
1. GAID's voice engine may make a copy of this person's voice from the recordings above.
2. The copy **will speak GAID's answers** about the works at the galleries in
   section 5, including wording written by **Amazon Nova, an AI model**, from
   the gallery's own words. The estate will not approve each sentence in advance.
3. **GAID will not announce**, aloud or on screen, that the voice is a copy.
4. The copy will **never** speak words sent by a member of the public.
5. Every clip carries an **inaudible watermark**.
