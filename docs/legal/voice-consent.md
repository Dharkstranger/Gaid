## 2. What we will make

From recordings of you speaking, GAID's voice engine makes a **voice ID**: a
copy of your voice that can say new sentences you have not recorded.

## 3. What the copy will say, and how

By signing, you agree that:

1. The copy of your voice **will speak GAID's answers to visitors' questions**
   about the works at the gallery or galleries named in section 6. Some of
   those answers are written by **Amazon Nova, an AI model**, from the
   gallery's own words. You will not approve each sentence in advance.
2. **GAID will not announce**, aloud or on screen, that the voice is a copy.
3. GAID will **never** make your voice say words sent by a member of the
   public. It only speaks answers GAID's own server produced and signed.
4. Every clip GAID makes in your voice carries an **inaudible watermark**, so
   a clip can be identified later as made by GAID.

## 4. What we will hold, and why (Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023)

| What | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Your recordings (held-back pieces only, for re-testing) | To check the copy still sounds like you | Until you withdraw |
| The ten-second reference and the voice ID | To speak in your voice | Until you withdraw |
| Your recorded consent sentence | Proof that you agreed, and that it was you | Until you withdraw |
| This form | Proof that you agreed | Six years after you withdraw, then deleted |

A recording of your voice may be **sensitive personal data** (biometric
data) under the Act. The lawful basis is **your explicit consent**, which you
may withdraw at any time (section 7). The recordings are stored on GAID's own
voice engine, hosted by Modal Labs, Inc. (United States). They are never sold,
and never used to train any model except the copy of your voice described here.

**Your rights:** access, correction, deletion, objection and withdrawal of
consent. Write to the address in section 9. You may also complain to the
Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

**If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom:** the GDPR or UK
GDPR applies as well, with the same lawful basis (explicit consent, Article
9(2)(a)).

## 5. The likeness test

GAID uses the copy only if it passes the likeness test in `docs/voices.md`.
The rest of your recording is held back, and the copy must sound at least 90%
as much like you as you sound like yourself. A copy that fails is deleted,
and nothing of it is kept.

## 7. Withdrawing

You may withdraw at any time, for any reason, without penalty:
- tell us at the address in section 9, or tell the gallery;
- the gallery marks the voice as withdrawn and publishes, and **GAID stops
  using it within one minute**;
- we then **delete** the voice ID, the reference, your recordings and your
  recorded consent sentence from the engine within 7 days, and confirm to you
  in writing. Only this signed form is kept, as the table in section 4 says.

Clips already played to visitors cannot be recalled.
